Privacy policy

Velox Mobile · Updated November 2, 2026

How the app handles your Salesforce connection, device data and optional support requests.

Your Salesforce connection

Velox Mobile is an independent Salesforce administration and developer tool. You sign in to an existing Salesforce organization through Salesforce OAuth. The app does not create a separate Velox account and has no developer-operated backend. Your Salesforce organization and identity provider handle authentication under their own policies. Velox Mobile does not read or store your Salesforce password.

Data processed and stored

The app sends requests, code and changes you choose to execute to your connected Salesforce organization. Salesforce and your organization may retain records, audit trails and logs according to their policies. Connection details, saved queries, query and REST history, Apex snippets, preferences and diagnostic errors are stored on your device. OAuth credentials are kept in platform secure storage. The app's additional token store on iOS uses device-only Keychain access. Other local data may be included in operating-system backups. Protect saved code and queries as they may contain confidential information.

Authentication identifiers

Signing in to Salesforce uses your account information, including your username or email address and Salesforce user and organization identifiers. The Salesforce mobile SDK also sends a platform-specific device identifier during authentication: the vendor identifier on iOS and the Android ID on Android. These identifiers support account access and app functionality; Velox Mobile does not use them for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Sharing and external services

There is no advertising, tracking or developer analytics. Salesforce SDK analytics are disabled. There is no automatic upload of diagnostic errors to the developer. Salesforce requests and sign-in may use Salesforce services and your organization's identity provider. Opening a browser, exporting files, copying data or sharing diagnostics transfers the content you select to the destination you choose; that destination's privacy policy applies. Review exported content before sharing it.

Notifications

Optional notifications are generated locally when the app observes deployment changes or high organization limits. Monitoring depends on the app running and having a network connection. It is not a continuous background monitoring service. You can control notification permission in device Settings.

Removing connections and data

Remove an organization in Settings to remove its local connection and credentials. The app also attempts to revoke its Salesforce session; network failures may prevent server-side revocation. Saved queries, snippets and history are retained until you choose Delete All Device Data. That option removes all connected organizations, app-owned preferences, saved content, history, local diagnostic logs, local notifications and temporary metadata exports. It does not delete your Salesforce user account, records, remote logs, device backups or copies you exported elsewhere. Your Salesforce administrator can revoke connected-app access and manage data held by your organization. Do not rely on uninstalling the app to erase iOS Keychain credentials; remove connections or delete device data first.

Support and privacy requests

The publisher responsible for Velox Mobile is Giohanna Hervias Ballon. For support or privacy questions, contact spectralabsdev@gmail.com.

If you email support, your email address and the content you choose to send are processed to respond to your request. Do not include Salesforce passwords, tokens or confidential records. You can request deletion of support correspondence, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

This website

These support pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. We have not added advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies to these pages. The app’s Salesforce data is not sent to this website.

Changes to this policy

Updates will be identified with a new effective date. For requests about data held in Salesforce, contact your organization’s administrator; the app publisher does not control your Salesforce organization.
