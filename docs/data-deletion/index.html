Velox Mobile / Data controls

Remove your local data.

Velox Mobile connects to an existing Salesforce account. It does not create a separate Velox account.

Remove a connection

Remove an organization in Settings to remove its local connection and credentials. The app also attempts to revoke its Salesforce session; network failures may prevent server-side revocation. Saved queries, snippets and history remain until you delete all device data.

Delete all device data

Choose Settings → Delete All Device Data. The action is also available on the login screen. This removes app connections, saved content, history, preferences, diagnostic logs, local notifications and temporary metadata exports.

It does not delete Salesforce accounts or records, remote logs, operating-system backups or copies exported elsewhere. Remove connections or delete device data before uninstalling; iOS Keychain credentials can survive uninstalling.

Data held by Salesforce

Contact your Salesforce administrator to revoke connected-app access or request changes to your account or organization data. The app publisher does not control your Salesforce organization.

Support correspondence

To request deletion of correspondence sent to support, email spectralabsdev@gmail.com. Applicable retention obligations may limit deletion. Do not include passwords, tokens or customer records.
